# HLFM Open House AR

An augmented reality showcase built for the **Ngee Ann Polytechnic Open House**, presenting work by students of the **Hotel and Leisure Facilities Management (HLFM)** course.

> **Please note:** This project was created for Open House demonstration purposes. The content featured here (designs, concepts, models and other materials) is the original work of HLFM Students' work to showcase their learning. It is not a commercial product and is not intended for reuse or redistribution.

---

## About

This experience lets Open House visitors step into student projects through mixed reality. Using a **Microsoft HoloLens 2**, visitors can view and interact with student work placed in the space around them, giving a hands-on look at what HLFM students create across their modules.
